Request account deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your IQ7 Notes account and associated personal data, including if you can no longer access the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to iqnotestesting8@gmail.com with the subject IQ7 Notes Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email address associated with your IQ7 Notes account, if you know it. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or payment card details.
  3. We may ask you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.
  4. We will review the request, process eligible account and personal-data deletion, and contact you if additional steps or legally required retention apply.

Email an account deletion request

What data is deleted?

After account ownership is verified and deletion is processed, we aim to remove or de-identify personal account information, such as your account profile and associated personal notes or uploaded content, where deletion is technically possible and not subject to a valid retention requirement.

Marketplace listings may be deactivated. Content that another customer has already purchased may need to remain available to that customer under their existing purchase entitlement.

What data might be retained?

Limited purchase, payment, refund, settlement, fraud-prevention, dispute, or audit records may need to be retained where necessary for legal compliance, financial reconciliation, security, or to preserve an existing buyer's access to purchased material. We aim to minimize personal information in retained records and restrict their use to those purposes.

Retention depends on the type of record and applicable obligations. Contact us at the address above if you would like information about records retained for your particular request and the applicable retention period.

What happens next?

Submitting a request does not instantly erase your account. Some requests require identity verification and a review of purchases, buyer access, or outstanding financial records. We will not describe your account as deleted until deletion has actually been completed.

You can request account deletion without installing or opening IQ7 Notes by using the email link above.
