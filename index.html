Study Notes – Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Study Notes is an educational mobile application designed to help users organize study notes, study subjects, use AI-powered search, summarize PDF documents, and access educational books.

1. Information You Provide

Study Notes may process information that you voluntarily enter or select while using the app, including study notes, subject names, questions entered into AI Search, and PDF documents selected for PDF AI summarization.

2. AI Search

When you use AI Search, the question you submit is sent through the Study Notes backend to an AI service so that an answer can be generated. Please do not enter passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive personal information into AI questions.

3. PDF AI

If you use PDF AI, the PDF you select may be transmitted to the Study Notes backend for processing and summarization. You should only upload documents that you are permitted to process and should avoid uploading documents containing highly sensitive personal information unless necessary.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Study Notes can store study notes and certain application settings locally on your device. The application does not require an account or password for its core note-taking features.

5. Information Collected Automatically

Study Notes does not intentionally request your name, phone number, contacts, precise location, or photographs for its core functionality. Internet services, hosting providers, and third-party services used to provide AI functionality may process technical information such as network information or request metadata as part of providing their services.

6. Third-Party Services

Study Notes uses third-party infrastructure and services to provide certain features, including backend hosting, AI processing, Project Gutenberg content/services for educational books, and internet/network services required to communicate with these services. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Advertising

Study Notes does not currently contain advertisements and does not intentionally use advertising services for displaying ads.

8. Children's Privacy

Study Notes is an educational application intended primarily for students and general educational use. It is not specifically directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

9. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by Study Notes and its associated services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

Information stored locally by the application remains on the user's device until the user deletes it, clears the application data, or uninstalls the application. Information transmitted to backend or third-party services may be retained or processed according to the policies and technical practices of those services.

11. Your Choices

You can choose whether to use features such as AI Search and PDF AI. You can delete notes stored within the application. You should not submit sensitive personal information through AI Search or PDF AI.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Study Notes changes its functionality, services, or privacy practices. The updated version will be published at the location where this policy is made available.
